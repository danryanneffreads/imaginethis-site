About Career Coordinates

A coach who actually knows how organizations work.

Career Coordinates is a Madison, Wisconsin-based career coaching and professional development practice serving individuals and organizations across the country. Whether you're navigating a career transition, feeling stuck in a role you've outgrown, building your leadership capacity, or learning to work with your neurodivergent brain rather than against it, this work is built around you.

Erich George brings more than 20 years of HR leadership experience inside Wisconsin's public and private sectors, including directing civil service systems and leading large-scale recruiting operations. That insider knowledge shapes every coaching conversation.
