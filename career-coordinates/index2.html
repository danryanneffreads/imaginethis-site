About Career Coordinates

A coach who knows
how organizations
actually work.

Career Coordinates is a Madison, Wisconsin-based coaching practice serving individuals and organizations across the country. Whether you're navigating a career transition, feeling stuck, building leadership capacity, or learning to work with your neurodivergent brain — this work is built around you.

Erich George brings more than 20 years of HR leadership inside Wisconsin's public and private sectors, including directing civil service systems and leading large-scale recruiting operations. That insider knowledge shapes every coaching conversation.
